ps5slop runs PS5 games on Apple Silicon Macs. It runs a game's own x86-64 code, implements the system libraries on the Mac (the way Wine does for Windows) and translates the GPU's commands to Metal.

You supply your own games, dumped from your own console. Nothing from Sony is part of the project.
